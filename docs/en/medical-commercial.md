# Acceptance for licensed medical and residential care in China

[Documentation](README.md) · [中文](../medical-commercial.md) · [Deployment](quick-deploy.md) · [Operator manual](workspace-manual.md)

Before commissioning, confirm which clinical services the institution is licensed to provide, who is qualified to provide them and what each supplier will deliver. Use a separate instance and keys for each contracted institution. Clinical and residential care have different requirements. In the November 2026 review, the applicable comparisons were mandatory GB 38600—2019 for service safety and recommended WS/T 876—2026 for health management services. The latter took effect on September 1, 2026. [Integrated-care management guide](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/c100175/202010/accd70521a3444be9e4983c5a64cf0fd.shtml) · [GB 38600—2019](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=D8AB02F0141FE11A4976E0E94FCF58B4) · [Health standards announcement](https://wsbz.nhc.gov.cn/wsbzw/article/7/2026/3/409002819b90d7ad019d04d301a60065.html)

| Area | Implemented controls | Evidence required at commissioning |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and authority | Each institution has a separate database. Staff roles, branch scope, named approvals and revocation govern access. | Verify staff licences and the least-privilege matrix. Check that other branches and family accounts are denied restricted records and that the termination procedure removes access. |
| Retention | At the residential one-year review, licensed-medical mode retains the archived resident’s identity, links and original documents. | Assign a records officer to classify encounters and document the relevant clinical dates, legal holds and approved disposal. |
| Audit | For protected clinical reads, the audit records the person, time, resource, result and connection peer. Database triggers block ordinary changes and deletions to the audit log. | Check both allowed and denied requests against the proxy logs. Test export permissions and watermarks. Retain immutable audit copies under independent control. |
| Agents and suppliers | Agents use tools within the caller’s scope. The workflow can resume from a checkpoint. Staff review the draft and confirm the work. | Assess model, OCR, ASR, messaging and backup providers. Test integrations with fictional records first and rehearse the emergency response without AI. |
| Recovery | The installation uses SQLCipher, separate keys, encrypted snapshots, restore checks, signed upgrades and pre-upgrade verification. | Recover on a clean host and reconcile originals, cash records and audit history. Measure recovery time and recovery point. Check how the operating procedures work during a network or power outage. |
| Devices and clinical systems | Registered device credentials, bridge heartbeats, permissions and staff checks | For each device model, check missed events, false alarms, timestamps and replay after an outage. Reconcile patient identity, clinical signature, timestamp, sealing and copying with the authoritative medical record system. |

For Chinese medical institutions, the retention clock for outpatient and emergency electronic records in institutional custody starts at the last clinical visit. The minimum is 15 years. For inpatient electronic records, keep them for at least 30 years from the last hospital discharge. Checking out of residential care does not establish either clinical date. When the residential year expires, licensed mode keeps the clinical links and queues the record for review. Records staff determine the legal class and retention period, including any dispute holds or special rules.

Continue to sign statutory records in the institution’s accepted HIS/EMR until formal acceptance covers the clinical record, signature, trusted time, watermark, sealing, copying and integration workflows. A staff member’s workflow confirmation is distinct from a legally validated electronic signature. [National electronic record requirements](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/mohwsbwstjxxzx/s8553/201702/ebe31b47c1ea4bcfaed973fb8806992c.shtml) · [2025 clinical information access notice](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202507/01/531972/article.html)

Medical and health data are sensitive personal information. Document why the institution needs the data, the legal basis and scope of processing, the notices people receive and who can access the records. Obtain separate consent where applicable. Complete the required personal information protection impact assessments for sensitive processing, outsourcing, disclosure and international transfers. Send only hospital or city names in map queries. The language and map provider chosen for a deployment do not determine which law applies.

Determine the applicable Chinese cybersecurity protection level and obtain the required filings and assessments. Retain evidence of remediation and supplier acceptance. Software test results document engineering work. Certification and legal compliance require review at the site, and deployments in other jurisdictions need their own review. [Personal Information Protection Law](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm) · [Network Data Security Regulation](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2024-09/30/c_1729384452307680.htm) · [Cybersecurity measures for medical institutions](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/bgt/c100251/202302/96218ad117b54853afdaccd38323d7af/files/1733795759650_44285.pdf)

In China, retain residential care records for at least five years after the service agreement expires. Removing a record from everyday use triggers a retention review. The records officer can list pending cases locally with `manage_deployment.py review --db PATH --category retention`. Use `--category reads` for clinical access receipts, and `--after` and `--limit` to page through results. [Article 32, Measures for the Administration of Elderly Care Institutions](https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/flfggz/flfggzbmgz/202101/t20210104_146537.html)
