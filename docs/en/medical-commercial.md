# Acceptance for licensed medical and residential care in China

[Documentation](README.md) · [中文](../medical-commercial.md) · [Deployment](quick-deploy.md) · [Operator manual](workspace-manual.md)

Reviewed in November 2026. Establish the institution's licensed clinical scope, staff qualifications and supplier responsibilities before commissioning. Use a separate instance and keys per contracted institution. Clinical services and residential care have distinct requirements. Compare the site's service safety with mandatory GB 38600—2019 and its health management services with recommended WS/T 876—2026, effective September 1, 2026. [Integrated-care management guide](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/c100175/202010/accd70521a3444be9e4983c5a64cf0fd.shtml) · [GB 38600—2019](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=D8AB02F0141FE11A4976E0E94FCF58B4) · [Health standards announcement](https://wsbz.nhc.gov.cn/wsbzw/article/7/2026/3/409002819b90d7ad019d04d301a60065.html)

| Area | Implemented controls | Evidence required at commissioning |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and authority | Separate institution database, staff roles, branch scope, named approval and revocation | Verify staff licenses, least-privilege matrix, denial across branches and family accounts, termination procedure |
| Retention | Licensed-medical mode preserves archived resident identity, links and originals at the residential one-year review date | Assign a records officer, classify encounters, document the relevant clinical dates, legal holds and approved disposal |
| Audit | Protected clinical reads record actor, time, resource, result and connection peer. Database triggers prevent ordinary audit updates and deletions | Verify permitted and denied cases, correlate proxy logs, test export authorizations and watermarks, retain externally controlled immutable audit copies |
| Agents and suppliers | Scoped tools, reviewable drafts, checkpoints and human confirmation | Assess model, OCR, ASR, messaging and backup providers. Exercise emergency response without relying on AI and test integrations with synthetic records first |
| Recovery | SQLCipher storage, separate keys, encrypted snapshots, restore checks, signed upgrades and pre-upgrade verification | Recover on a clean host. Reconcile originals, cash records and audit history. Measure recovery time, recovery point and operating procedures during network or power loss |
| Devices and clinical systems | Registered device credentials, bridge heartbeats, permissions and staff verification | Test each model for missed events, false alarms, timestamps and offline replay. Reconcile the patient identity, clinical signature, timestamp, sealing and copying with the authoritative medical record system |

For medical institutions in China, outpatient and emergency electronic records under institutional custody have a minimum retention period of 15 years from the last clinical visit. Inpatient electronic records have a minimum of 30 years from the last hospital discharge. A residential checkout date alone cannot establish either date. Licensed mode places records in a review queue when the residential year expires and preserves the clinical links. Records staff determine the relevant legal class and retention period and check for dispute holds or special rules. Continue signing statutory records in the institution's accepted HIS/EMR until the clinical record, signature, trusted time, watermark, sealing, copying and integration workflows have undergone formal acceptance. Distinguish staff workflow confirmations from legally validated electronic signatures. [National electronic record requirements](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/mohwsbwstjxxzx/s8553/201702/ebe31b47c1ea4bcfaed973fb8806992c.shtml) · [2025 clinical information access notice](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202507/01/531972/article.html)

Medical and health data are sensitive personal information. Document the legal basis, purpose, necessary scope, notices and access rules. Obtain separate consent where applicable and carry out required personal information protection impact assessments for sensitive processing, outsourcing, disclosure and international transfers. A deployment's language and map provider do not determine the applicable law. Map queries should contain hospital or city names only. Determine the applicable Chinese cybersecurity protection level, obtain required filings and assessments, and retain remediation and supplier acceptance evidence. Software tests provide engineering evidence. Certification and legal compliance require site-specific review. Other jurisdictions require their own review. [Personal Information Protection Law](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm) · [Network Data Security Regulation](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2024-09/30/c_1729384452307680.htm) · [Cybersecurity measures for medical institutions](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/bgt/c100251/202302/96218ad117b54853afdaccd38323d7af/files/1733795759650_44285.pdf)

Chinese residential care records must be retained for at least five years after the service agreement expires. Retirement of a record from the day-to-day workflow triggers a retention review. The records officer can list pending cases locally using `manage_deployment.py review --db PATH --category retention`. Use `--category reads` for clinical access receipts and page with `--after` and `--limit`. [Article 32, Measures for the Administration of Elderly Care Institutions](https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/flfggz/flfggzbmgz/202101/t20210104_146537.html)
