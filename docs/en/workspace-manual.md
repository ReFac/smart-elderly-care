# Institution workspace manual

[Documentation](README.md) · [中文版](../workspace-manual.md) · [Health assistant and project agent handbook](agent-handbook.md) · [Desktop menu and screenshot](../desktop-navigation.md)

Sign in at the address your institution publishes. Staff use `/?workspace=1`; on the host machine the default address is `http://localhost:8765`. Use the account your institution issued. The first Owner sets a password during installation. English deployments show English labels. This guide includes the Chinese labels in parentheses so colleagues working across editions can find the same screen.

## Choose an entry point

| Role and immediate task | Where to start | What to check afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception, enquiries | Reception and admission (接待入住) → Reception (接待管理) → Manual entry (手工登记) | Customer follow-up and the appointment calendar |
| Reception, create a resident | New resident record (新建长辈档案) below the institution name or on Home (首页) | A waiting record in Admission (入住管理) |
| Care worker, start a shift | Care services (照护服务) → Shift handover (班次交接), then Care tickets (照护工单) or Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) | Signed tickets and the resident's timeline |
| Nurse or clinician, review findings | Vitals (健康体征), Alerts and confirmations (预警与确认), or Resident records (长辈档案) → Medical information (医疗资料) | Disposition, signed encounter, order or risk report |
| Finance, collect or refund money | Fees (费用管理) → Financial overview (财务总览), Unpaid bills (待收费), Receipt history (收费记录) | Source bill, cash ledger and refund reconciliation |
| Owner, administer or restore | System administration (系统管理) → Accounts and permissions (账号与权限), Institution settings (机构设置), or System monitoring (系统监控) | Audit trail, Recent changes and a verified recovery point |
| Any authorised user, locate a command | Top-right Health assistant (健康助理), or AI in the sidebar | Click the response's button and check the destination and filters |

Your account permissions, campus and assigned rooms determine which menu entries you see. Expand a sidebar group and use the business view selector (业务视图) to reach its other ledgers. You can keep ten work tabs open, including Home. Click the pin to keep a tab open. Pinned tabs are highlighted. When you open an eleventh tab, the workspace can replace the least recently used unpinned tab with no unsaved changes. If every tab is protected, choose one to close.

Open a resident's linked name and check the room and record ID. If two employees share a name, check their position, branch and account. In the confirmation dialog (确认改动), check whom the change affects, its dates and amounts, and what will happen. You can add a comment. If someone has changed the record since you opened it, read the current record and receipts before submitting again.

## Visitors, enquiries and appointments

1. **Public environment tour.** Visitors can go straight to `/visit`, `/roomtour` or `/faq` without an account. Staff can find Environment tour (环境导览) and FAQ (常见问题) in the sidebar. After five seconds of active tour viewing, visitors who have not signed in can dismiss the invitation or share contact details for a callback. WeChat number sharing requires a configured institution mini-program and the visitor's explicit authorisation. If it fails, the visitor can enter the number manually. Ordinary visitors can register with a username and password. The visit request collects its own contact details. [Client and tour instructions](../clients.md)
2. **Record a real contact.** For a call or walk-in, open 接待管理. Enter the actual time, phone number, subject and feedback. Check the source and whether the number belongs to a new customer or a known resident or relative. You can make a manual booking with a number already on file. Call the visitor to confirm their intentions and record the outcome in Customer follow-up (客户跟进). The callback queue takes account of admissions and requests to stop follow-up.
3. **Allocate a seat.** After confirming by phone, open Reception calendar (接待时间表). Choose the campus, day, appointment type (visit or callback), start time, duration and seat. The *same seat* must be free for every half-hour of the booking. Open the entry to reschedule, cancel or record attendance and feedback. Archive (归档记录) keeps completed contacts from the last 365 days. [Reception and scheduling](../facility.md)

## Admissions and beds

| Workflow | Enter and review | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create a record | In 新建长辈档案, enter the resident's name, sex, date of birth or age, personal mobile, campus and record category. Expand the sections you need for languages, marital status, former occupation or administrative addresses. With the appropriate grants, you can add a spouse, children, contact purposes, verification dates, financial support and pension, or sign an initial health summary. | Review once to save the record and all its entries together. Waiting residents have no bed assignment. If an archived resident has the same name, check the record ID. A new stay may have its own record. |
| Trial and conversion | Choose 办理试住 on the resident's row. Check the dates, bed and one-time charge. The Owner can change the initial three-day, two-night default under 试住约定. Use 试住 to view the trial and 办理入住 to convert it. | The bed shows 试住中. The resident stays on the waiting list until staff record a decision. Expiry releases the bed. An outstanding decision triggers reminders. If the trial does not lead to admission, the follow-up task records the contact, reason and outcome. |
| Formal stay and renewal | Choose an existing record and a vacant bed. Check the move-in date, monthly billing day, term, first-period charges and deposit. An open-ended term is available. Verify how the first payment will be covered. | Confirm sufficient advance funds, a verified payment or explicit approval for temporary admission in arrears. Creating a record alone leaves the bed vacant. By default, bills fall due on the day of the month the resident moved in. If that day does not exist in a short month, the bill falls due on the last day. Later months return to the agreed day. Eligible settled renewals extend a fixed term. |
| Transfers, leave and discharge | Search by part of a name or room number. Select the new room or bed on the map or by number. For a transfer, check the building, room and bed at both ends. Enter the actual dates and reason under Leave (请假管理), or confirm discharge with a summary. | A transfer frees the old bed and preserves the original location on past work. Leave keeps the bed assigned. Discharge frees it. Check Discharges (退住记录), the archived record and the separate financial settlement. |
| Card and retention | Open the detailed record from the title bar. Use the bed card (床头卡) or other supported print actions. | A scanned card is checked against the current assignment and account. Reprint after a transfer. At the one-year review, formal institutions preserve files and originals for the records officer. The demo cleans up everyday personal data. Licensed medical deployments have additional preservation controls. |

**Bed management (床位管理).** Start with the campus, building and floor. The bed ledger, all-beds view, room board and floor map show the same assignments. Sort by floor and room, room number, care area or map position. Administrators can add buildings, floors and rooms, change room capacity and edit the floor layout. Resolve conflicts with occupied beds before changing capacity. In 区域设置, build the area's hierarchy within one building, assign floors or individual rooms and give each staff member one or more care areas. Their account permissions determine which records they can access. [Facility administration](../facility.md)

## Read a resident's record

- **Registration (登记资料).** Here you can check identity documents, household contacts, address, sources of funds and pension. Look in the financial records for actual charges. To file identity originals, open the document form or subsidy materials screen. To request a temporary edit, identify the resident and give a reason. If they are outside your list, enter their full record ID in the request in Personal centre. An eligible real-name approver reviews the request under 预警与确认 → 权限申请. Approval permits one edit to that resident's basic registration within 24 hours. [Account and request rules](../access.md)
- **Medical information (医疗资料).** Check confirmed chronic conditions and their sources, allergies, medicines, assessments and past encounters. To add an encounter by hand, record its date, hospital, prior medicines, prescription and instructions. To use the assistant, upload originals from one encounter and find the resident by name or room. Select the previous encounter to supplement it, or set the date of a new one. Before signing, check the OCR text against the original pages, including the hospital and department. Keep CT images as originals and take written findings from the report. While the draft is being prepared, you can continue the conversation. Reopen **Document tasks** in the assistant to check its status. The receipt on a finished draft shows the runtime used and the task ID. Open a medical assessment to see its report, item scores, earlier assessments and print options. [Registration and encounters](../dossier.md)
- **Institution timeline (疗养院时间线).** Choose a day to open a meal, care event or receipt. Authorised staff can enter a health introduction themselves or review a draft based on that resident's authorised records. Request recent changes and suggestions as needed or generate them through the rounds policy. Ask the staff involved to check missing or conflicting observations. Open Recent changes from inside the record to keep the list filtered to this resident. [Care timeline](../care-timeline.md) · [Rounds and observations](../rounds.md)

## Day-to-day care

| Entry under 照护服务 | What the operator does | Where the evidence appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ability assessment (能力评估); Safety risk assessment (安全风险评估) | Choose the resident, instrument, assessment date and two actual assessors for the standard scale. For a paper source enter its reference. Record observed scores, evidence for any adjustment, and the reassessment date. Use the specific risk form for its relevant observations. | Current level, dated assessment, scoring breakdown and review date. Earlier results remain available. |
| Care plans (照护计划); Reassessment (复评安排) | Review the proposed support items. Check service package, frequency, responsible staff/role and enabled items, then obtain authorised approval. | Effective plan and associated tasks. A change to an active plan goes through review again. |
| Care desk (照护工作台) and Care tickets (照护工单) | Open a pending ticket and check the resident and bedside assignment. Scan the bed card where applicable. Record what you did or saw and confirm. Review completed work and spot-check its evidence. | Completed services (已完成服务) and the room and resident timelines show who completed the task and at which bed. Expand grouped water or meal entries to see individual receipts. Unusual events and medication rounds each have a separate entry. |
| Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) | Select the dates, weekdays, time and assignee for a meal, inspection or care schedule. Choose the campus, rooms or named residents it covers. Set weekday menus and use 当天餐单 for a one-day change. | Today's dispatch (今日派发) lists the actual recipients and tickets. The demo schedules three meals and two inspections. Pausing stops future dispatches. Rerunning on the same day avoids duplicates and can catch up after a same-day restart. |
| Group activities (集体活动); meal batches | Verify actual attendance, remove absentees, uncheck skipped dishes and record individual portions. The authorised lead confirms. | Click a name in the signed batch to open what that resident actually ate. Subsequent menu changes affect unsigned tickets. |
| Vitals (健康体征); Screening and follow-up (筛查与随访) | Record value, unit and observed exception. Review the evidence and record disposition. For follow-up, document observations, basis for priority, owner and next date, then record actual outcome. | Vital receipt, open follow-up or completion record. A shared-room signal needs verified individual attribution. |
| Shift handover (班次交接) and Staff scheduling (人员排班) | Choose the branch, day, employee and shift. Check the weekly roster and full shift ledger. Record the handover issues and name the recipient. The recipient signs in with their own account. | The ledger shows whether a handover is pending or signed, the two people involved and their times. If a handover is missing, check the assignment and your access. |
| External care referral (院外衔接) | Specify the receiving facility, channel and information. Record the person's sharing consent and evidence, generate an encrypted handoff package, then record the actual receiving person and receipt number. | Dated consent, revocation, generated file and receipt. Automatic HIS and public-health delivery need separate integration. |

**Medication.** Register medicines and quantities received for a resident in 药品接收登记 under reception. In 用药管理, enter an existing order's drug, instructions, responsible person and due time. Before administration, check allergy alerts, possible duplicates and the institution's approved interaction rules, then sign the review. Open 定时提醒 and 筛查提醒 to see which orders are due and the evidence behind an alert. Authorised staff maintain the sources and versions of interaction rules in 配伍规则. You can look up or correct a medicine the resident reported at an examination. The original self-report and the clinician's order each keep their own history.

**At the bedside.** On the room page choose 核对本房服药. You can also tell the assistant the room, bed, time of day and any exceptions. Confirm each resident and each time slot that has started separately. For a missed dose, record the actual reason. You can leave an item for later. Saying “No medication needed today” creates a staff report for nursing review. Staff with the required grants may also mark a due order that has had an independent review. Check the signed entries in the resident's record. [Assessment and referrals](../care-pathways.md) · [Schedules](../scheduled-work.md) · [Bedside medication](../medication-rounds.md)

**Alerts and confirmations (预警与确认).** Open the device event and check the room and circumstances. Verify in person, then record the resident involved, what you found and what you did. For a spoken request, compare the transcript with the draft and either confirm it or send it back. A confirmed proposal leaves the shared queue and keeps its receipt. This screen also holds rounds reminders, permission requests and staff announcements. The Owner sets the rounds interval and delivery window. A verified cough count needs the observation time, coverage and evidence identifying the resident. [Device review](../integrations.md) · [Rounds](../rounds.md)

## Supplies, finances and reports

- **Warehouse (仓库管理).** Enter the item and unit in 库存台账. You can add the brand, model or package. Review similar items before choosing an existing entry or creating a separate one. Record the quantity and transaction details whenever stock comes in or goes out. Compare 出入库流水 with the balance. For a 护理用具工单, assign a person and record how the request was fulfilled.
- **Bill preparation.** In 月度账单确认 review the earliest outstanding billing period, rates, room changes, care level, leave, meals and agreed additions. Confirm the line items. 待收费 → 查看明细 shows charges, allocations, paid amount and balance. 医疗费用 surfaces relevant charges. Record actual advance receipts in 预缴余额; verify prior balances, application to a bill, deposit and any excess payment separately. Record real collection follow-up in 欠费提醒.
- **Collection and settlement.** For an offline receipt, record the actual method and gross amount. For a channel payment, enter the amount, create a merchant order and display its WeChat or Alipay QR code or use the configured scanner or terminal. Check the signed notification and order status. If the outcome is unclear, query the existing order. The details show gross payment, the fee the institution bears and the net deposit. Check the order, channel transaction ID, date and allocations in 收费记录 / 实收流水 and 渠道对账. The Owner maintains the fee rates.
- **Refunds.** Find the original receipt in 收费记录. Check the refundable balance, including amounts pending or already refunded, then select the reason, channel and partial or full amount. In 退款对账, query the original request and verify the channel's result. For an offline refund, verify its evidence and record it separately. Repeated channel notifications should post once. On departure, open 退住结算 to check advances, deposits, outstanding bills and the actual refund. Reconcile 日结报表 with that day's transactions. [Finance and channel procedures](../finance-reports.md)
- **Expenses.** 财务总览 → 支出登记 supports paying-party evidence, amount, recipient and purpose or a preview of bank/Henan electricity files. Download the source-specific CSV example, verify debit direction, settlement status, dates and duplicate candidates, then confirm selected rows. The file workflow works independently of a bank-to-enterprise feed or electricity-provider API.
- **Subsidy preparation (民政补贴申报材料).** Filter application month, new/renewal, building and floor. Check ID faces, household documentation and invoiced months for each person. Maintain originals and eligible-month invoices, or adjust preparation rules when authorised. Select eligible rows to generate and print a versioned list. Record an external authority's actual receipt separately.
- **Reporting and insurance.** Open 统计报送 → 经营月报 for the collections, expenses, tasks and inventory you can access. Select dates and comparison periods for the detail. In 民政报送, generate a draft, resolve validation issues and export the encrypted package. Record what you actually submitted and the receipt you received. Preserve the submitted version when correcting returned data. In 长期护理保险 → 登记办理, record the person, jurisdiction, stage, document reference and benefit dates. Review earlier applications, assessments, decisions and reassessments there. Transmission to government systems depends on arrangements with the receiving authority. ## Recovery, updates and delivery checks

1. Open 机构设置 → 备份与恢复 to see scheduled, manual, pre-restore and pre-update recovery points. The scheduler takes a full, compressed and encrypted snapshot every day at midnight Shanghai time. After downtime, it captures the state available on restart. Verify the actual capture time, retention and local destination. For remote copies, check each verification result. iCloud, 115 and Baidu require storage provisioned by your institution.
2. Select a recovery point to export its `.akbak`. To import one, upload the file, authenticate the institution and file, review the differences and add it to the list. Applying it to the live database requires a second confirmation and creates a protection point. If the import conflicts with protected receipts, medical originals or signatures, read the blocker list or restore to an isolated directory to inspect the data. Keep the database key and off-host recovery materials. [Backups and recovery](../backups.md) · [Storage](../storage.md)
3. For 平台更新 inspect the candidate, download and verify it, then choose 备份并安装. A trusted signing key, release source and managed launcher are required. Automatic updates use a configurable window. Check the pre-update point, startup result and any rollback record. The Electron host uses the upgraded service; distribution of a signed native shell has its own release process. [Updates](../updates.md)
4. If a page will not load, check the local or published address, the server and the port. If one view has disappeared, check your effective permissions and scope. After a network interruption, read the original ticket, bill, payment order or change history before submitting the same change again. Staff can still use the manual care and admission forms during an optional AI provider outage.

For a licensed medical deployment, you can enable preservation with `NH_INSTITUTION_PROFILE=licensed-medical`. Check which profile is running before testing retention or recovery. Use a separate Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`) institution with fictional residents for acceptance. Exercise the Chinese interface and shared admission, bed, care, medication, receipt and recovery functions in that database.

Live supplier checks cover text-message and email delivery, cloud storage, payment settlement, merchant terminals, signed updates, Roomtour assets and AI services. The platform has adapter contracts for Mi Home, radar and smart badges. Staff can review synthetic suspected falls and prolonged bed rest in the workspace. Physical-device delivery, multi-stage emergency calls, external notifications, HIS exchanges, full pharmacy inventory and expiry processing, and offline nursing entry each have separate implementation and acceptance requirements. Mobile and WeChat releases also need appropriate domains, credentials and checks on physical devices.
