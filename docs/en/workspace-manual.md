# Institution workspace manual

[中文版](../workspace-manual.md) · [Health assistant and project agent handbook](agent-handbook.md) · [Desktop menu and screenshot](../desktop-navigation.md)

English deployments use English interface labels. Chinese labels in parentheses help staff working across editions locate the same business view. Open the institution's published address for staff access; the workspace path is `/?workspace=1`. On the host machine the default URL is `http://localhost:8765`. Use an account issued by the institution. The first Owner sets a password at installation.

## Choose an entry point

| Role and immediate task | Where to start | What to check afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reception, enquiries | Reception and admission (接待入住) → Reception (接待管理) → Manual entry (手工登记) | Customer follow-up and the appointment calendar |
| Reception, create a resident | New resident record (新建长辈档案) below the institution name or on Home (首页) | A waiting record in Admission (入住管理) |
| Care worker, start a shift | Care services (照护服务) → Shift handover (班次交接), then Care tickets (照护工单) or Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) | Signed tickets and the resident's timeline |
| Nurse or clinician, review findings | Vitals (健康体征), Alerts and confirmations (预警与确认), or Resident records (长辈档案) → Medical information (医疗资料) | Disposition, signed encounter, order or risk report |
| Finance, collect or refund money | Fees (费用管理) → Financial overview (财务总览), Unpaid bills (待收费), Receipt history (收费记录) | Source bill, cash ledger and refund reconciliation |
| Owner, administer or restore | System administration (系统管理) → Accounts and permissions (账号与权限), Institution settings (机构设置), or System monitoring (系统监控) | Audit trail, Recent changes and a verified recovery point |
| Any authorised user, locate a command | Top-right Health assistant (健康助理), or AI in the sidebar | Click the response's button and check the destination and filters |

The menu depends on the signed-in person's grants, campus and room scope. Expand the sidebar group, then use the business view selector (业务视图) for additional ledgers. The upper tab strip holds up to ten work tabs including Home. Click the pin to keep a tab; pinned tabs have their own highlight. At capacity, the least recently used unpinned tab without unsaved changes can give up its place. If all tabs are protected, close one explicitly. Click a resident's linked name and verify the room and record ID. For employees with the same name, check their position, branch and account. In the confirmation dialog (确认改动), review the person, dates, amounts and consequences; add a comment if useful. After a version conflict, re-read the current record and receipts before submitting again.

## Visitors, enquiries and appointments

1. **Public environment tour.** On the staff sidebar the public resources are Environment tour (环境导览) and FAQ (常见问题). Visitors can open `/visit`, `/roomtour` or `/faq` without signing in. After five seconds of active tour viewing, an anonymous visitor may close the callback invitation or choose to share contact details. The WeChat flow needs the institution's configured mini-program and the visitor's explicit authorisation. Manual entry is available if number authorisation fails. Ordinary visitors can register with a username and password; contact details for a visit request are collected separately. [Client and tour instructions](../clients.md)
2. **Record a real contact.** On 接待管理, staff record an incoming call or walk-in with the actual time, phone, subject and feedback. Review the source, new-customer marker or known resident/family relationship. Previously known numbers are valid for a manual booking. Call to confirm the visitor's intentions, then record the outcome in Customer follow-up (客户跟进). Admission or a refusal to receive follow-up affects the callback queue.
3. **Allocate a seat.** In Reception calendar (接待时间表), choose campus, day, visit or callback, time, duration and reception seat. Book only after telephone confirmation. Every half-hour of the appointment must be free on the *same* seat. Scattered free slots on different seats cannot cover one continuous booking. Reschedule or cancel from its entry; record actual attendance and feedback. Completed contacts remain available in Archive (归档记录) for the latest 365 days. [Reception and scheduling](../facility.md)

## Admissions and beds

| Workflow | Enter and review | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create a record | 新建长辈档案 collects name, sex, date of birth or age, personal mobile, campus and record category. Open optional sections for languages, marital status, former occupation and administrative addresses. Authorised users may stage a spouse, children, contact purposes, verification dates, financial support and pension, or sign an initial health summary. | One employee confirmation saves the completed record and its staged entries together. A waiting resident has no assigned bed. Check the record ID if an archived resident has the same name. A new stay can retain a separate record from the prior stay. |
| Trial and conversion | In the resident row choose 办理试住. Review dates, bed, and one-time price. The Owner sets defaults under 试住约定. The starting default is three days and two nights. Use 试住 in the row to view details, then 办理入住 to convert. | A trial bed shows 试住中. The resident remains on the waiting list pending a decision. An expired trial releases the bed; unresolved decisions raise reminders. A failed conversion produces follow-up work with contact, reason and outcome. |
| Formal stay and renewal | Choose an existing record and an available bed. Verify move-in date, monthly billing day, term, first-period charges, deposit and how payment is covered. An open-ended term is supported. | Confirm available advance funds, verified payment or an explicitly approved temporary admission in arrears. New records alone occupy no beds. The default billing day is the day of admission; a short month uses its last day and later months resume the agreed day. Eligible settled renewals extend a fixed term. |
| Transfers, leave and discharge | Search by part of a name or room number. Click a room or bed on the map, or choose it by number. For a transfer verify the old and new building, room and bed. Record actual dates/reason under Leave (请假管理) or confirm discharge and a summary. | A transfer releases the old bed; historical work retains its execution location. Leave holds the bed. Discharge releases it; review Discharges (退住记录), the archived record and separate financial settlement. |
| Card and retention | Open the detailed record from the title bar. Use the bed card (床头卡) or other supported print actions. | A scanned card is checked against the current assignment and account. Reprint after a transfer. At the one-year review, formal institutions preserve files and originals for the records officer. The demo cleans up everyday personal data. Licensed medical deployments have additional preservation controls. |

**Bed management (床位管理).** The bed ledger, all-beds view, room board and floor map use the same assignments. Select campus, building and floor first. Sort by floor/room, room number, care area or map position. Administrators can extend buildings, floors and rooms, change room capacity and maintain the floor layout. Resolve occupied-bed conflicts before a capacity change. Under 区域设置, define a building-specific hierarchy, assign floors or individual rooms, then assign one or more care areas to staff. Actual access remains governed by account permissions. [Facility administration](../facility.md)

## Read a resident's record

- **Registration (登记资料).** Check identity documents, household contacts, address, sources of funds and pension. Actual charges have their own financial records. Use the document form or subsidy materials screen for identity originals. If temporary permission is needed, request a specific resident with a reason. An eligible real-name approver reviews under 预警与确认 → 权限申请. If the resident is outside your list, use the request in Personal centre and enter their full record ID. An approved basic-registration edit applies to that one resident and can be used once within 24 hours. [Account and request rules](../access.md)
- **Medical information (医疗资料).** Review confirmed chronic conditions, their source, allergies, medicines, assessments and encounter history. Add a manual encounter with date, hospital, prior medicines, prescription and instructions, or upload one encounter's originals through the assistant. Search the resident by name or room, choose a previous encounter for a supplement or set a new encounter date. Compare OCR with original pages and the hospital/department before signing. CT images remain originals; a written finding comes from a report. Open the medical assessment to check the report, item-level detail, previous assessments and print options. [Registration and encounters](../dossier.md)
- **Institution timeline (疗养院时间线).** Select a day and open a meal, care event or receipt. Authorised staff can enter a health introduction or review a draft based on this resident's authorised records. Recent changes and suggestions can be generated on demand or under the rounds policy. Verify missing or conflicting observations with the staff involved. Recent changes within a resident record retains that person's filter. [Care timeline](../care-timeline.md) · [Rounds and observations](../rounds.md)

## Day-to-day care

| Entry under 照护服务 | What the operator does | Where the evidence appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ability assessment (能力评估); Safety risk assessment (安全风险评估) | Choose the resident, instrument, assessment date and two actual assessors for the standard scale. For a paper source enter its reference. Record observed scores, evidence for any adjustment, and the reassessment date. Use the specific risk form for its relevant observations. | Current level, dated assessment, scoring breakdown and review date. Earlier results remain available. |
| Care plans (照护计划); Reassessment (复评安排) | Review the proposed support items. Check service package, frequency, responsible staff/role and enabled items, then obtain authorised approval. | Effective plan and associated tasks. A change to an active plan goes through review again. |
| Care desk (照护工作台); Care tickets (照护工单) | Open a pending ticket, check resident and bedside assignment, record actual execution or observations, then confirm. Scan a bed card when applicable. Review finished work and sample its proof. | Completed services (已完成服务), room and resident timelines preserve operator and execution bed. Expand grouped water/meal entries to see each receipt. Unusual events and medicine rounds retain separate entries. |
| Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) | Create a meal, inspection or care schedule; select dates, weekdays, time, campus/rooms/named residents and assignee. Set weekday menus, or override a single date using 当天餐单. | Today's dispatch (今日派发) shows actual recipients and tickets. The demo has three meals and two inspections. Pausing affects future dispatches; the same-day run is deduplicated and can catch up after a same-day restart. |
| Group activities (集体活动); meal batches | Verify actual attendance, remove absentees, uncheck skipped dishes and record individual portions. The authorised lead confirms. | Click a name in the signed batch to open what that resident actually ate. Subsequent menu changes affect unsigned tickets. |
| Vitals (健康体征); Screening and follow-up (筛查与随访) | Record value, unit and observed exception. Review the evidence and record disposition. For follow-up, document observations, basis for priority, owner and next date, then record actual outcome. | Vital receipt, open follow-up or completion record. A shared-room signal needs verified individual attribution. |
| Shift handover (班次交接); Staff scheduling (人员排班) | Select the correct branch, day, employee and shift. Check the weekly roster and full shift ledger. Record handover issues, nominate the recipient, then have that person sign in with their own account. | The ledger distinguishes pending and signed handovers, with both people and times. Check scope or assignment when a handover is unavailable. |
| External care referral (院外衔接) | Specify the receiving facility, channel and information. Record the person's sharing consent and evidence, generate an encrypted handoff package, then record the actual receiving person and receipt number. | Dated consent, revocation, generated file and receipt. Automatic HIS and public-health delivery need separate integration. |

**Medication.** Use 药品接收登记 in the reception group to register a resident's incoming medicines and quantities. Under 用药管理, enter an existing order with drug, instructions, responsible person and due time. Review allergy alerts, pending duplicates and institution-approved interaction rules, then sign the review before administration. Use 定时提醒 and 筛查提醒 to find due orders and their evidence. Authorised staff maintain versions and sources in 配伍规则. Patient-reported medicines from an examination may be looked up and corrected; the self-report and the clinician's order remain separately traceable.

**At the bedside.** Choose 核对本房服药 on the room page, or tell the assistant the room, bed, time of day and exceptions. Confirm each person and each started time slot separately. Record an actual reason for a missed dose, or skip an item for later. “No medication needed today” becomes the staff member's report for nursing review. Only users with the required grants may also tick an independently reviewed, due order. Review signed entries in the resident record. [Assessment and referrals](../care-pathways.md) · [Schedules](../scheduled-work.md) · [Bedside medication](../medication-rounds.md)

**Alerts and confirmations (预警与确认).** Open the underlying device event, inspect room and context, verify the situation in person and record actual resident, conclusion and disposition. For speech proposals compare the transcript with the draft, confirm or return it. Completed proposals leave the shared queue and retain a receipt. This module also contains rounds reminders, permission requests and staff announcements. The Owner configures the rounds cadence and delivery window. Verified cough counts require observation time, coverage and evidence identifying the resident. [Device review](../integrations.md) · [Rounds](../rounds.md)

## Supplies, finances and reports

- **Warehouse (仓库管理).** In 库存台账, enter the item, unit and optional brand/model/package. Review suggested similar items and either use an existing entry or create a distinct item. On receiving or issuing stock, record quantity and transaction details; check 出入库流水 against the balance. For 护理用具工单, choose the responsible person and record the fulfilment outcome.
- **Bill preparation.** In 月度账单确认 review the earliest outstanding billing period, rates, room changes, care level, leave, meals and agreed additions. Confirm the line items. 待收费 → 查看明细 shows charges, allocations, paid amount and balance. 医疗费用 surfaces relevant charges. Record actual advance receipts in 预缴余额; verify prior balances, application to a bill, deposit and any excess payment separately. Record real collection follow-up in 欠费提醒.
- **Collection and settlement.** For an offline receipt enter the actual payment method and gross amount. For a channel payment, staff set the amount and create the merchant order; display its WeChat/Alipay QR code or use the configured scanner/terminal. Verify the signed notification and order status. On uncertainty query the existing order. Gross payment, institution-paid fee and net deposit are separately displayed. Open 收费记录 / 实收流水 and 渠道对账 to verify order, channel transaction ID, date and allocations. The Owner maintains the applicable fee rates.
- **Refunds.** Select the original receipt in 收费记录, review refundable amount, reason and channel, then request a partial or full refund. Inspect pending and previously refunded amounts. Under 退款对账 query the original request and verify the channel result. Record separately verified offline refunds using their evidence. Repeated notifications should post once. On departure use 退住结算 to check advances, deposits, open bills and the actual refund. Compare 日结报表 with the day's transactions. [Finance and channel procedures](../finance-reports.md)
- **Expenses.** 财务总览 → 支出登记 supports paying-party evidence, amount, recipient and purpose or a preview of bank/Henan electricity files. Download the source-specific CSV example, verify debit direction, settlement status, dates and duplicate candidates, then confirm selected rows. The file workflow works independently of a bank-to-enterprise feed or electricity-provider API.
- **Subsidy preparation (民政补贴申报材料).** Filter application month, new/renewal, building and floor. Check ID faces, household documentation and invoiced months for each person. Maintain originals and eligible-month invoices, or adjust preparation rules when authorised. Select eligible rows to generate and print a versioned list. Record an external authority's actual receipt separately.
- **Reporting and insurance.** 统计报送 → 经营月报 shows authorised collections, expenses, tasks and inventory; drill into dates and comparison periods. In 民政报送 generate a draft, address validation issues and export its encrypted package; register actual submission and receipt. Amend returned data and protect the submitted version. 长期护理保险 → 登记办理 records the person, jurisdiction, stage, document reference and benefit dates; review earlier applications, assessments, determinations and reassessments. Government transmission requires the receiving authority's arrangements. [Financial reporting](../finance-reports.md) · [Insurance records](../dossier.md)

## Recovery, updates and delivery checks

1. Under 机构设置 → 备份与恢复, inspect scheduled, manual, pre-restore and pre-update recovery points. The current scheduler captures an encrypted, compressed full snapshot daily at midnight in Shanghai time; after downtime it captures the state available on restart. Verify actual capture time, retention, local destination and remote verification. iCloud, 115 and Baidu destinations need the institution's provisioned remote storage. See the actual status of each copy.
2. Export a chosen `.akbak`. To import, upload it, authenticate the institution and file, preview differences, then add it to the recovery-point list. Applying it to the live database is a separate confirmation and creates a protection point. If protected receipts, medical originals or signatures conflict, consult the blocker list or restore to an isolated directory for inspection. Preserve the database key and off-host recovery materials. [Backups and recovery](../backups.md) · [Storage](../storage.md)
3. For 平台更新 inspect the candidate, download and verify it, then choose 备份并安装. A trusted signing key, release source and managed launcher are required. Automatic updates use a configurable window. Check the pre-update point, startup result and any rollback record. The Electron host uses the upgraded service; distribution of a signed native shell has its own release process. [Updates](../updates.md)
4. If the page fails to load, verify the correct local or published address, server availability and port. Check effective scope if a view disappears. After a network interruption, consult the original ticket, bill, payment order or change history before retrying a write. Manual care and admission forms remain available when an optional AI provider is unavailable.

Clinical deployments can enable preservation using `NH_INSTITUTION_PROFILE=licensed-medical`. Verify the actual deployment profile before a retention or restoration exercise. Acceptance uses a separate Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`) institution and fictional residents. Exercise the Chinese interface and shared admission, bed, care, medication, receipt and recovery functions in that database. Test live supplier dependencies separately, including text-message/email delivery, cloud storage, payment settlement, merchant terminals, signed updates, Roomtour assets and AI services. Mi Home, radar and smart-badge capabilities include adapter contracts; synthetic suspected fall/prolonged-bedrest review is available in the workspace. Physical-device delivery, multi-stage emergency calls, external notifications, HIS exchanges, full pharmacy inventory/expiry processing and offline nursing entry have separate implementation and acceptance requirements. Mobile and WeChat release also require appropriate domains, credentials and physical-device checks.
