# Health assistant and agent handover handbook

[Documentation](README.md) · [中文版](../agent-handbook.md) · [Institution workspace manual](workspace-manual.md) · [Owner deployment console](deployment-console.md)

Staff can look up a record and open the form they need in the same conversation. Each specialist drafts its answer from the records that role may read. Operators can reuse reviewed community skills, connect tools through the official MCP SDK and offer other agents selected read-only queries about the institution.

DeerFlow runs staff conversations and prepares drafts from the evidence authorised for each task. Expand the receipt on an answer or a completed draft to see which runtime processed it. For deployment responsibilities, access checks and staff sign-off, see the [component and maintenance guide](agent-components.md).

## Using the assistant as a member of staff

Open **AI** in the sidebar, **Health assistant** (健康助理) at the top right, or the floating assistant icon. Each opens the same conversation. When a reply offers a button, click it to open the business page. The assistant stays beside it in a smaller window where you can keep asking questions. You can drag the window. The larger window remembers its position.

Press Enter to send or Shift + Enter for a new line. To speak, hold the speech button and release it. The assistant transcribes, cleans up and sends what you said. Select Read aloud (朗读) to hear replies to spoken questions. Use the playback window to pause, resume or exit. Before you confirm a form, compare names, room numbers, amounts and exceptions such as “except for” with the transcript. [Conversation and voice](../agent-runtime.md)

| Task | Example in the Chinese edition | What to do with the answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare for handover | 「今天怎么样了？」 (How are things today?) followed by 「过去 48 小时呢？」 | Check which period the answer covers, which events staff recorded and what remains to be done. The usual 24-hour summary also reports current figures such as bed occupancy. A nurse with access can ask which exceptions are new and how many residents have observations on record. |
| Check receipts and debt | 「这个月流水多少？今天收了多少，还欠多少？」 | In 收费记录, finance staff can compare receipt dates and vouchers with month-to-date receipts, today’s collections and the outstanding balance. Count an advance once, when received. Applying it to a bill must not count it again. |
| Open a filtered work page | 「打开床位管理，筛选主楼」 or 「如何发送定时批量报单？」 | Click the returned Bed management (床位管理) or Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) button. Check the building and tab. For similarly named buildings, include the branch and building name. |
| Assign a task | Ask 「今天护理用具够吗」 to check stock, then 「创建护理用具工单」 when ready | A stock enquiry returns information. When you ask to create a ticket, open it for review, choose an assignee, check quantities and publish. You need the corresponding publishing permission to announce it institution-wide. |
| Confirm a medicine round or meal | 「101、102 房的长辈已完成早晨服药，除了……」 or 「今天午餐大家都吃了，某某没吃鱼」 | Check each resident, bed, round, exception and reason. Confirm medicine intake for each person. Follow the resident link in the meal record to verify actual dishes and the staff member who signed. |
| Find instructions or public information | 「怎样办理试住？」 or 「找一下养老机构防跌倒指南」 | Use the FAQ (常见问题) for workspace guidance. Enable the web switch for public care topics and inspect links returned with search results. Use authorised facility records for questions about a person's chart or the institution's cash flow. |
| Open a form | 「新增员工」「登记生命体征」「给这位长辈办理试住」「登记院务支出」 | Open the proposed form and fill in its required fields. After you confirm, look for the entry in the register, receipt or audit trail. The form uses your account’s business permissions. |

On an English deployment, try “How are things today?”, “Create a resident record” or “Record vital signs”. To open a page, try “Open bed management” and select the button. The daily overview also recognises “Comment va la résidence aujourd’hui ?” for French institutions and “¿Cómo está hoy la residencia?” for Spanish institutions. Each summary draws on the records available to the signed-in account.

The assistant can read records for your branch and rooms within your account’s grants. When asking about one resident, give their name, record ID and bed. Check health summaries against that person’s own sources. A resident without measurements cannot count towards observation coverage.

The model uses at most the last five eligible, complete exchanges in the current scope. Older entries may still appear on screen. If the input would exceed the budget, the oldest complete exchanges drop out first. At a shift or scope change, give the dates and name the person or branch you mean. ### Prepare records from photos, PDFs or workbooks

1. Ask 「帮我填写一下这个老人的个人信息吧，一张图片可以吗？」 (Can you help me fill in this resident's details from a photo?). Click Upload resident materials (上传长辈资料) if your role can edit the record, or use Upload images (上传图片) directly in chat. Select 1–12 JPEG, PNG, PDF, XLS or XLSX files, up to 8 MiB per file and 24 MiB per group. Finish or close the current group before choosing another.
2. Check each suggested category against the original, correct it if needed and select Confirm classification (确认分类). Registration papers, identity documents, résumés, examination reports and prescriptions go to different forms. If digits are unclear or text is missing, check the source page. An ID card can belong in both the registration and subsidy files. Choose the destination for this task.
3. **Registration.** Search by name or room in the list you have permission to use. Match the person and bed, then compare the saved and recognised values, including the choices in dropdowns and the address. If the name in the document differs from the record, check the identity again. For a new resident, choose 用这份资料新建档案. Review the full form and open the family, contact or health fields you need before saving. To keep an original in the credential or subsidy file, open that destination separately, check the resident, document type and period, and save the original there.
4. **Clinical encounter.** Group the documents for one resident’s visit. Select the resident and either an existing encounter or a new encounter with its date. Check the hospital, extracted notes, written prescriptions or instructions and attachments. Images without a written interpretation need clinical review. Once signed, the encounter appears in 长辈档案 → 医疗资料.
5. **Staff résumé and contract.** Confirm the document category and open the new-employee form for a résumé. Review the supported original details, including name and contact information. An authorised staff member selects the role and permissions before saving. For a contract select the existing employee and confirm the filing destination.
6. **Workbook.** Download the registration template, or download a workbook containing the fields already recognised from an original. In WPS, Excel or LibreOffice keep headers in row one and use one resident per row. For a multi-sheet workbook, name the registration sheet `Resident registration` or `长辈登记`. Each import accepts up to 500 data rows and 64 columns. Supported fields include name, birth date, phone, language, marital status, education, former occupation, native place, birthplace and current address. Select a row, verify the source and resident and resolve formula or option warnings before saving. Uploading or downloading a workbook alone creates no admission or charge. [Upload and workbook instructions](../assistant-harness.md)

**If a step fails.** An answer with no filing button can mean your account lacks the assistant grant or the permission for the destination. If two residents have the same name or an expected record is missing, check the branch, assignment and record ID. Eligible staff can request temporary access. An authorised approver must sign under their real name.

If an upload fails, check the file type, size and current group. You can also enter details in the destination form. For a provider failure, keep the original and note the request ID and time. Ask the Owner to check 机构设置 → AI 用量 and the service status. The institution subscription, upstream account balance, media settings and network connection can each affect the request. Check the ledger before you submit it again. [Workspace permissions](workspace-manual.md) · [Provider and usage](../provider.md)

### Schedule a resident’s health review and daily living summary

| Where | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| System management → Institution settings → Scheduled rounds (定时巡查) | As Owner, open the schedule for Health changes (长辈健康变化) or Daily life (今日生活) under 长辈档案自动整理. Enable or pause each one separately, enter an interval of 5–1,440 minutes and confirm the agent and interval. System monitoring → Scheduled tasks also links to these settings. |
| Generation and notification settings on the same page | Choose the institution’s default generation mode and the time for in-app notifications. In the resident’s detailed record, open Facility timeline → Recent changes → Generation settings to inherit the default or choose automatic or manual generation. Both schedules respect the resident’s selection. |
| Resident summary or detailed record → Facility timeline | Read dated drafts under Recent changes. Daily life uses confirmed attendance, meals and care records. Health changes requires medical and clinical permissions and draws on observations, vital signs and attributable health records. Expand the evidence for each item to check its date, type and source ID. Entries from earlier days retain their original dates. |
| Checking progress | Look at the last result, next run and pending jobs. The pending count includes jobs waiting for a retry. Each draft gives its date and source or model. If a result is missing, check the resident’s assignment, generation mode and schedule, then refresh the queue. Verify or enter any missing observations. |

Only authorised staff can view and review clinical drafts. Prescriptions, diagnoses, task signatures and contact with a family each follow their own procedure. A daily life update can reach a family member when the resident consents and that recipient subscribes.

### Extend with an existing MCP or community skill

1. Install `requirements-harness.txt` in the service's Python environment. Open 机构设置 → AI 套餐与识别服务 → 查看助理工具与技能, or inspect the Owner-only `GET /api/assistant/extensions`, to see what this installation loads. Ask 「多伦多现在几点」 and verify the time zone and tool result. Spreadsheet export invokes a separate Excel MCP process limited to that request's temporary directory.
2. Check first whether a community server already supports the capability. Register the server and each allowed tool in the operator-controlled `NH_MCP_CONFIG`. Give each tool its required permissions, keywords and permitted arguments. The connector supports stdio and Streamable HTTP. Remote services need HTTPS and explicit operator trust. Main chat offers tools only when the operator approves them and the server declares them read-only. Use the scoped business APIs for facility operations. Treat tool output as data. Keep credentials, medical documents and arbitrary file paths out of public lookups. [Configuration example](../examples/workspace-mcp.json) · [Harness setup](../assistant-harness.md)
3. Point `NH_AGENT_SKILL_DIRS` at reviewed skill directories. The host reads `SKILL.md` and its relevant Markdown references when needed. Check the upstream version, licence and intended roles. The bundled skills cover internal communications, XLSX preparation and resident records. The skill describes how to do the work. The caller still needs the business permission, and a field proposed after OCR needs evidence from the original. [Bundled sources and licences](../../skills/UPSTREAM.md)
4. Deployments can select DeepSeek, OpenAI-compatible chat, Anthropic or Google. OCR and public web search currently use DeepSeek. ASR and TTS can independently use DeepSeek, OpenAI or a self-hosted HTTP adapter. Test actual media input, partial output, provider credit and usage receipts per service. Commercial customers use institution plans. Hosted clients and trusted operator deployments have different settings rights. [Deployment modes](deployment-console.md) · [Provider configuration](../provider.md)

## Reproduce an agent workflow

1. Sign in with an account that can read inventory and ask 「核对库存，然后计算三份每份 2.55 元的总价」 (check inventory and calculate the total for three units at CNY 2.55 each). The model can look up inventory, read the result and use the decimal arithmetic tool. Open **Tools used** to see which tools ran and how long they took. For each call, the server checks the tool and its arguments against the current account scope. A staff message can use up to three tool calls in total, including follow-up reads. Each account and assignment has up to five turns of conversation history for context. The encrypted institution database stores long-lived operational facts and checkpoints.
2. Generate a briefing that covers care and inventory. After an interruption, retry with the same request ID. The checkpoint preserves the work of specialists who finished. A new source revision or loss of the required permission invalidates the run. The evaluator checks figures and claims about completed actions and allows one correction. A staff member approves the briefing for publication. The workflow draws on tool observations, persistent graphs and supervisor/specialist coordination. [Workflow patterns](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) · [Persistence](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence)
3. Use a dedicated staff account to send an [A2A task](agent-protocol.md). Retrieve it after a restart and check that a different account is denied. For a new MCP integration or skill, verify its allowed actions, arguments, grants, transmitted data and timeouts. Treat source documents and external tool output as data.
4. In the server’s Python environment, run `python tools/evaluate_agent.py`. For a smaller run, select `--group chat`, `workflow`, `tools` or `documents`. The harness uses temporary databases, simulated model replies and real SDK clients. In the Chinese edition it tests authorisation, budgeting, upstream failures, retries, human review, checkpoint recovery and document handling. Use independent, authorised cases to measure factual accuracy and how often staff make corrections. Unlimited plans still record usage. Reconcile calls when the provider receipt is uncertain.
