## Using the assistant as a member of staff

Open AI in the sidebar, Health assistant (健康助理) at the top right, or the floating assistant icon. They lead to the same conversation. Click a button in an answer to open its business page. The assistant shrinks to a draggable companion window for follow-up questions. The larger floating window remembers its position. Press Enter to send text or Shift + Enter for a new line. Hold the speech button to record, then release to transcribe, clean up and send. Select Read aloud (朗读) to hear answers to spoken questions. The playback window supports pause, resume and exit. Check names, room numbers, amounts and exceptions such as “except for” against the transcript and the review form. [Conversation and voice](../agent-runtime.md)

| Task | Example in the Chinese edition | What to do with the answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare for handover | 「今天怎么样了？」 (How are things today?) followed by 「过去 48 小时呢？」 | Check the time window, recorded events and outstanding work. The usual 24-hour activity summary also identifies current snapshots such as bed occupancy. An authorised nurse can ask about newly recorded exceptions and how many residents had observations. |
| Check receipts and debt | 「这个月流水多少？今天收了多少，还欠多少？」 | Finance staff can reconcile month-to-date receipts, money collected today and outstanding balances against receipt dates and vouchers in 收费记录. Applying an existing advance to a bill must not count as a second receipt. |
| Open a filtered work page | 「打开床位管理，筛选主楼」 or 「如何发送定时批量报单？」 | Click the returned Bed management (床位管理) or Scheduled tickets and batch dispatch (定时工单与批量派发) button. Check the building and tab. For similarly named buildings, include the branch and building name. |
| Assign a task | Ask 「今天护理用具够吗」 to check stock, then 「创建护理用具工单」 when ready | Click the review action, choose an assignee, verify quantities and publish. Asking about stock returns information. An institution-wide announcement requires the corresponding publishing grant. |
| Confirm a medicine round or meal | 「101、102 房的长辈已完成早晨服药，除了……」 or 「今天午餐大家都吃了，某某没吃鱼」 | Check each resident, bed, round, exception and reason. Confirm medicine intake for each person. Follow the resident link in the meal record to verify actual dishes and the staff member who signed. |
| Find instructions or public information | 「怎样办理试住？」 or 「找一下养老机构防跌倒指南」 | Use the FAQ (常见问题) for workspace guidance. Enable the web switch for public care topics and inspect links returned with search results. Use authorised facility records for questions about a person's chart or the institution's cash flow. |
| Open a form | 「新增员工」「登记生命体征」「给这位长辈办理试住」「登记院务支出」 | Click the proposed action. Fill the required fields yourself, confirm in the form, and verify the result in its register, receipt or audit trail. The current account's business permissions apply. |

On an English deployment, try “How are things today?”, “Create a resident record” or “Record vital signs”. To open a page, try “Open bed management” and select the button. The daily overview also recognises “Comment va la résidence aujourd’hui ?” for French institutions and “¿Cómo está hoy la residencia?” for Spanish institutions. Each summary draws on the records available to the signed-in account.

**What the model can see.** Data access follows the questioner's branch, rooms and grants. Identify an individual by name, record ID and bed. Check that health summaries refer to that person's own source material. A resident with no measurements cannot count toward observation coverage. The assistant uses at most the last five eligible complete exchanges in the current scope. Older conversation entries may remain visible in the interface. When input reaches its budget, older complete exchanges drop out first. Give the dates and the intended person or branch explicitly when starting another shift or changing scope. ### Prepare records from photos, PDFs or workbooks

1. Ask 「帮我填写一下这个老人的个人信息吧，一张图片可以吗？」 (Can you help me fill in this resident's details from a photo?). Click Upload resident materials (上传长辈资料) if your role can edit the record, or use Upload images (上传图片) directly in chat. Select 1–12 JPEG, PNG, PDF, XLS or XLSX files, up to 8 MiB per file and 24 MiB per group. Finish or close the current group before choosing another.
2. Compare each suggested category with the original. Correct it if needed, then click Confirm classification (确认分类). Registration material, identity documents, résumés, examination reports and prescriptions have different destinations. Review the original page for unclear digits and missing text. An ID card may be relevant to both registration and a subsidy file. Choose the destination required for this task.
3. **Registration.** Search the authorised list by name or room, then select the actual person with the matching bed. Compare previous and recognised values, including enumerated fields and addresses. A conflict between the name on the original and the selected record requires a new identity check. For a new person choose 用这份资料新建档案, review the single full form and expand family, contact or health fields as appropriate. Staff save the verified record. To retain evidence in the credential or subsidy file, open that separate destination, verify the resident, document type and period, then save the original there.
4. **Clinical encounter.** Use one set of materials for one resident's visit. Select the resident, choose an existing encounter or create one and supply its date. Review the hospital, extracted notes, written prescriptions or instructions and attachments. After signing, find the encounter in 长辈档案 → 医疗资料. Images without a written interpretation require clinical review.
5. **Staff résumé and contract.** Confirm the document category and open the new-employee form for a résumé. Review the supported original details, including name and contact information. An authorised staff member selects the role and permissions before saving. For a contract select the existing employee and confirm the filing destination.
6. **Workbook.** Download the registration template, or download a workbook containing the fields already recognised from an original. In WPS, Excel or LibreOffice keep headers in row one and use one resident per row. For a multi-sheet workbook, name the registration sheet `Resident registration` or `长辈登记`. Each import accepts up to 500 data rows and 64 columns. Supported fields include name, birth date, phone, language, marital status, education, former occupation, native place, birthplace and current address. Select a row, verify the source and resident and resolve formula or option warnings before saving. Uploading or downloading a workbook alone creates no admission or charge. [Upload and workbook instructions](../assistant-harness.md)

**When a step fails.** If there is an explanation without a filing button, check both the assistant grant and the action grant for the destination. If there are duplicate names or a resident is missing, verify branch, assignment and record ID. Eligible staff can request temporary access and an authorised approver must sign under their real name. For upload problems check type, size and the current group. The destination business form also supports manual entry. For a provider failure, keep the source, note the request ID and time, and ask the Owner to check institution AI usage (机构设置 → AI 用量) and service status. Subscription, provider account balance, media configuration and network availability can fail independently. Check the ledger before retrying a submission. Assistant actions and manual business entry points use the same role, branch and object checks. Start by identifying the staff action to reproduce. ### Extend with an existing MCP or community skill

1. Install `requirements-harness.txt` in the service's Python environment. Open 机构设置 → AI 套餐与识别服务 → 查看助理工具与技能, or inspect the Owner-only `GET /api/assistant/extensions`, to see what this installation loads. Ask 「多伦多现在几点」 and verify the time zone and tool result. Spreadsheet export invokes a separate Excel MCP process limited to that request's temporary directory.
2. Look for an existing community implementation for the proposed capability. Register the server and specific tools in the operator-controlled `NH_MCP_CONFIG`. Specify required permissions, keywords and permitted arguments per tool. Supported transports are stdio and Streamable HTTP. Remote services require operator trust and HTTPS. Main chat exposes explicitly approved tools that the server declares read-only. Facility operations continue through the scoped business APIs. Treat tool results as data. Credentials, medical documents and arbitrary file paths should not be passed into public lookups. [Configuration example](../examples/workspace-mcp.json) · [Harness setup](../assistant-harness.md)
3. Set `NH_AGENT_SKILL_DIRS` to reviewed skill directories. The host loads `SKILL.md` and relevant Markdown references on demand. Check upstream version, licence and intended roles. Included packages cover internal communications, XLSX preparation and this workspace's resident records. A skill guides an action, while the active business grant controls its availability. Preserve evidence for data proposed after OCR. [Bundled sources and licences](../../skills/UPSTREAM.md)
4. Deployments can select DeepSeek, OpenAI-compatible chat, Anthropic or Google. OCR and public web search currently use DeepSeek. ASR and TTS can independently use DeepSeek, OpenAI or a self-hosted HTTP adapter. Test actual media input, partial output, provider credit and usage receipts per service. Commercial customers use institution plans. Hosted clients and trusted operator deployments have different settings rights. [Deployment modes](deployment-console.md) · [Provider configuration](../provider.md)

## Reproduce an agent workflow

1. Sign in as a staff member with inventory access and ask 「核对库存，然后计算三份每份 2.55 元的总价」 (check inventory and calculate the total for three units at CNY 2.55 each). The model can use an authorized inventory read, observe its result and call the decimal arithmetic tool. Expand **Tools used** for names and timings. The server validates the offered tool, arguments, current scope and maximum call count. Conversation history is scoped to the account and assignment and bounded to five turns. Long-lived operational facts and checkpoints live in the encrypted institution database.
2. Generate a specialist briefing spanning care and inventory. Retry with the same request ID after interruption. Completed specialists resume from a checkpoint. Changing the source revision or removing the required permission invalidates the run. The evaluator checks numbers and claims of execution and allows one correction. A staff member approves publication. The design draws on tool observations, durable graph execution and supervisor/specialist workflows. [Workflow patterns](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) · [Persistence](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence)
3. Use a dedicated staff account to send an [A2A task](agent-protocol.md). Retrieve it after a restart and check that a different account is denied. For a new MCP integration or skill, verify its allowed actions, arguments, grants, transmitted data and timeouts. Treat source documents and external tool output as data.
4. Run `python tools/evaluate_agent.py` with the server's Python environment. Select `--group chat`, `workflow`, `tools` or `documents` for a smaller run. The harness uses temporary databases, simulated model responses and real SDK clients to test authorization, budgeting, upstream failures, retries, human review, checkpoint recovery and document handling in the Chinese edition. Use independent, authorized evaluation cases for factual accuracy and staff correction rates. Unlimited institutional plans still record actual usage. Calls with uncertain provider receipts require reconciliation.
